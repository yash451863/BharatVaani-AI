04 · FAQGOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A quick guide to what this current build can do.

Can I speak to Bharat Voice AI?

Yes. Use the microphone button in the composer, allow microphone access, speak, then review and send the transcription.

Which AI services power this experience?

Gnani Prisma handles speech recognition, Gemini generates chat responses, and Gnani Timbre generates speech audio.

Are Premium plans and payments available?

The plans shown here are a preview only. Accounts, subscriptions and payment processing are not enabled in this build.

Does it support every Indian language?

Language availability depends on the connected speech services and their configured models. The selector currently controls the preferred chat reply language.
